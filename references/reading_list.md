# Reading list: the craft of science

Not imported into CLAUDE.md (to save context). Books and articles to read, and ideas from them to improve this guide.
`papers.md` gives the papers that the guide uses now.

How to use it: mark the item when you read it. Write one line below it if the item must change the guide.
If it changes the guide, add the source to `papers.md`.

## Start here

- [ ] Box GEP (1976). *Science and Statistics.* [doi:10.1080/01621459.1976.10480949](https://doi.org/10.1080/01621459.1976.10480949). Worry about tigers, not mice.
- [ ] Hamming R. *The Art of Doing Science and Engineering*, and the talk *You and Your Research*. Work on important problems.
- [ ] McElreath R. *Statistical Rethinking.* What a model is, and what it cannot tell.
- [ ] Ramón y Cajal S. *Reglas y consejos sobre investigación científica.* The will, the method, and the faults of the scientist.
- [ ] Pérez Tamayo R. *¿Existe el método científico?* History and philosophy of science.
- [ ] Sennett R. *The Craftsman.* Good work for its own sake.

## Craft

- [ ] Buffalo V. *Bioinformatics Data Skills.* Robust and reproducible work.
- [ ] Hunt A, Thomas D. *The Pragmatic Programmer.* Habits of software builders.
- [ ] Ousterhout J. *A Philosophy of Software Design.* Complexity, modules, and comments.
- [ ] Schimel J. *Writing Science.* Writing as a part of thinking.

## Philosophy

- [ ] Medawar P. *Advice to a Young Scientist.* Science as the art of the soluble.
- [ ] Firestein S. *Ignorance.* Good questions are more important than data.
- [ ] Hacking I. *Representing and Intervening.* Are the entities in our models real?
- [ ] Mayo D. *Statistical Inference as Severe Testing.* A result is good if the test could refute it.
- [ ] Pirsig R. *Zen and the Art of Motorcycle Maintenance.* A novel about quality.

## Articles

- [ ] Levins R (1966). *The Strategy of Model Building in Population Biology.* A model cannot maximize generality, realism, and precision together.
- [ ] Platt JR (1964). *Strong Inference.* [doi:10.1126/science.146.3642.347](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.146.3642.347). Design the experiment that excludes hypotheses.
- [ ] Gelman A, Loken E (2013). *The Garden of Forking Paths.* Fix the rules before you see the results.
- [ ] Gelman A et al. (2020). *Bayesian Workflow.* [arXiv:2011.01808](https://arxiv.org/abs/2011.01808). What to do with models that do not converge or have many modes.
- [ ] Knuth D (1974). *Structured Programming with go to Statements.* The source of the motto of this guide.
